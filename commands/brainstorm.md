# Brainstorm

Structured ideation session with critical challenge. No implementation.

## Parameters

- **Topic** (required): problem, question, or design space to explore
- **Constraints** (optional): known boundaries, non-negotiables, or context

## Session rules

1. Challenge every assumption. Ask "why?" and "what if the opposite were true?"
2. Never produce implementation artifacts (code, configs, schemas). Stay at the idea level.
3. Actively surface cognitive biases (anchoring, sunk cost, confirmation bias, etc.) when detected.
4. Spawn research subagents when claims need evidence or alternatives need exploration.

## Workflow

1. Restate the problem; identify hidden assumptions in the framing.
2. Explore the solution space — diverge before converging.
3. For each promising direction, steel-man *and* stress-test it.
4. Summarize: ranked options, open questions, and next steps (still no implementation).

**Skill dependency:** `brainstorm`
